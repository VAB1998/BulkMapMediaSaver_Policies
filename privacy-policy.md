# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026

Bulk Map Media Saver does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information, browsing history, analytics, or user-created content.

The extension stores preferences and temporary download progress locally in Chrome storage. This data is used only to operate the extension and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

When a download is started, selected media is retrieved from Google-hosted URLs and saved to the user's computer. The extension does not upload or retain copies of downloaded media.
